Privacy Policy

Ledgerly for iPhone and iPad · Effective 9 October 2026

Ledgerly does not collect your data. There is no account and no server of ours for it to be sent to. The one thing that can leave your device is an optional backup to your own iCloud, and only when you ask for it.

What is collected

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no sign-in and no analytics. No usage statistics, no crash reports, no advertising identifier and no tracking of any kind.

Where your information lives

Everything you enter — accounts, balances, budgets, transactions and receipt photographs — is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is protected by your device passcode and is unreadable until the first unlock after your device starts.

If you have iCloud Backup switched on, your device backup may include the app's data, in the same way it includes other apps. That backup is between you and Apple; the developer has no access to it.

Optional iCloud backup

In More → iCloud backup you can copy your ledger to your own private iCloud database (CloudKit), under the Apple ID signed in on the device. Nothing is sent unless you choose Back up now. The records are handed to the system; the app opens no connection of its own. The database is private to your Apple ID: the developer cannot read it, and receipt photographs are not included. Restoring is never automatic and says what it will replace. Deleting the backup is done from iCloud settings on your device.

Receipts and Apple Intelligence

When you photograph a receipt, the image is read on your device using Apple's on-device text recognition and Apple Intelligence. The photograph, the amounts and the merchant are never uploaded. The app uses no external OCR service and no remote language model.

Who your information is shared with

Nobody. Not the developer, not an analytics provider, not an advertiser, not a bank. There are no third-party SDKs that collect anything. The app's only external dependency is GRDB, an open-source database library that runs entirely on your device.

Siri, Shortcuts and the share sheet

If you use the Siri, Shortcuts or share-sheet features, the text or image you hand to the app is processed on your device and stored with the rest of your data. The app cannot read your messages, your mail or your notifications, and iOS provides no way for it to do so — anything it sees is something you chose to give it.

Deleting your information

Deleting the app removes all of it from your device. Inside the app you can delete individual records at any time, and More → Storage & privacy → Delete unused receipt photos removes photographs of receipts you discarded. There is no server copy to request the deletion of, because there is no server.

Children

Ledgerly is a household budgeting tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, any change would be a clarification rather than a change in what happens to your data.

Questions

Questions about privacy, and anything else about the app, are handled through the App Store listing — see the support page. There is no mailing list and no address to be added to, because there is nothing collected to add you to.
