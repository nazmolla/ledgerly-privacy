Support

Ledgerly for iPhone and iPad

Feedback and problems go through the App Store: open Ledgerly's page in the App Store, tap Ratings & Reviews, and write what happened. The developer reads and replies there.

It helps to say which iOS version you are on and what you were doing when it went wrong. Most of what people ask is answered below first.

Common questions

Where is my data?

On your device, and only there. Ledgerly has no account, no server and no sync — see the privacy policy. If you delete the app, the data goes with it, so back your device up if it matters to you.

Receipt reading does nothing

Reading a receipt uses Apple Intelligence, which needs a supported device with it switched on in Settings. Where it is unavailable the review screen still opens and every field can be typed in by hand — nothing is blocked, it just is not filled in for you.

What is the "required float"?

How much has to stay in your chequing account so that everything due before your next pay clears. Money going out on a given day is assumed to clear before money coming in on that same day, because in practice it often does — so the figure is deliberately cautious.

Budget and the standing plan

The Plan screen is the standing budget: adding, changing or deleting a line there applies to every month. To give a single month its own amount, use the six-month view.

Can it read my bank messages automatically?

No — iOS does not allow any app to read your messages, mail or notifications. You can hand a bank alert to Ledgerly yourself: paste it on the transaction form, share it from Mail or Messages, or pass it in with a Shortcuts automation.

Reporting a bug

Through the App Store, as above. Include what you expected to happen and what happened instead — that pair is almost always enough to find it.

Because a review is public, please do not put real balances or anything else private in it. Describe the shape of the problem rather than the numbers.
